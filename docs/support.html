How Eight works

Eight takes your monthly income, sets your savings aside first, then subtracts your fixed bills and what you spend. The home screen shows what you've spent so far, the bills still to come, and how much is free to spend each day until payday.

Add spending with back tap

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  2. Tap Add Action, search for Open URLs and add it.
  3. Enter the link: eight://add for the iPhone app, or https://hellopickr.github.io/eight/#add for the website.
  4. Name the shortcut Add to Eight and tap Done.
  5. Open Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap, choose Double Tap, and pick Add to Eight.

Now a double tap on the back of your iPhone opens Eight straight to the keypad.

Log Apple Pay spending automatically

Eight can log every Apple Pay or Wallet card payment by itself, with the amount and the shop name. It runs entirely on your iPhone through the Shortcuts app, and Eight never sees your bank or card details. Open Profile › Log Apple Pay spending automatically in the app for the step-by-step setup. To stop it, delete the automation in the Shortcuts app's Automation tab.

Bills that add themselves

In Plan › Fixed bills, switch on Add to spent automatically for any bill that leaves your account by itself. Eight logs it on its due day. For other bills, tap Mark paid on the home screen when you pay them.

Savings and the end of the month

Each payday, Eight adds your monthly savings amount to your savings total. It then shows what you didn't spend last month and lets you add that to savings too.

Moving to a new phone

Eight keeps your data only on your device. To move it, open Profile › Copy backup on your old phone, send the text to yourself, then use Profile › Restore from backup on the new phone.

Deleting your data

Open Profile › Erase everything and start over, or delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.
