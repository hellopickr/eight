Eight

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Eight is a free budgeting app. Everything you enter stays on your own device. Eight has no accounts, no servers that store your information, no ads and no tracking.

What Eight stores

To work out your budget, Eight saves what you type into it: your income and payday, your savings goal, your fixed bills, the spending and money received that you log, your categories, and your currency. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage.

What Eight does not do

  • Eight does not send your budget, bills, spending or currencies anywhere.
  • Eight does not ask for your name, email, phone number, bank details or location.
  • Eight does not connect to your bank.
  • Eight has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not share or sell any information.

Exchange rates

To convert spending in other currencies, Eight downloads a public file of exchange rates about once a day, from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), with Cloudflare Pages (currency-api.pages.dev) as a backup. You can also refresh the rates yourself in Profile. The request is the same for everyone and contains none of your information: no budget, spending, currency or device identifier. As with any website, the server that answers sees your IP address. See the jsDelivr privacy policy and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

This is the only internet connection the Eight app for iPhone makes. Without a connection, Eight keeps working and uses the last rates it downloaded or its built-in rates.

The website

The web version of Eight at hellopickr.github.io/eight is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. As with any website, GitHub and Google receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests those files. Your budget data is never included in those requests. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Backups

In Profile, Copy backup copies your data to your device's clipboard so you can keep it somewhere yourself. Eight does not upload backups. Where you paste or store a backup is up to you.

Deleting your data

Open Profile and tap Erase everything and start over, or delete the app. Either removes all of Eight's data from your device. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Eight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
